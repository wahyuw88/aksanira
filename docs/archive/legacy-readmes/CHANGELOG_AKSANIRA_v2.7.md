# AKSANIRA Web App v2.7 — Conversion & Experience Improvements

## Implemented
- Homepage editorial cards now link to relevant full articles instead of the generic article index.
- Added a three-step product workflow on the homepage: budget agreement, vendor evaluation, and two-family coordination.
- Added clearly labelled illustrative data previews to all three hero product detail pages. Names, values, and dates are explicitly described as examples, not customer evidence or screenshots of the workbook.
- Added a purchase-clarity section to the catalogue describing deliverables, manual use, spreadsheet compatibility caveat, and checkout delivery expectations.
- Clarified that the three workbooks do not sync automatically and that the complete bundle has no standalone checkout until a real listing exists.
- Added contextual product CTAs to selected articles based on topic.
- Standardized second-person address on core product pages to formal Indonesian.
- Added responsive styles for preview tables and cards, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image alt text where missing, and reduced-motion support.

- No invented Family Command Center Mayar checkout, price, bundle price, discount, testimonials, customer logos, or usage metrics.
- Actual workbook screenshots still require screenshots captured from final Excel files. The new tables are illustrative previews, not workbook screenshots.
- Formspree inbox delivery, Mayar checkout/file delivery/refund terms, privacy-provider settings, domain SSL/redirect, production deployment, browser visual QA, Lighthouse, accessibility audit, and search indexing are not verified in this offline package.
